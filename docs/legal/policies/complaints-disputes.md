1. Purpose

This policy explains how to raise complaints and how DPM handles disputes on the platform.

2. DPM catalog orders

Contact us via the Contact page with your order number. We will investigate delivery, quality, and refund issues for orders paid to DPM.

3. Shop storefront orders

Step 1 — Contact the shop using details in your order.

Step 2 — If unresolved, use Report shop on your order page (you must have placed that order).

Step 3 — DPM reviews reports. We may caution or suspend a shop but cannot reverse MoMo/cash payments we did not process. Paystack disputes on shop accounts are handled under Paystack and the shop's policies.

4. Shop owners

If a customer dispute is unfair, respond with evidence in good faith. Repeated false customer reports may be referred to enforcement.

5. Response times

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few business days. Complex cases may take longer.

6. Limitations

DPM is not a court. Our decisions on platform access (warnings, suspension) are separate from any legal remedy you may pursue under Ghana law.

7. Escalation

Email or message via Contact with subject "Dispute — Order #…" including timeline and screenshots.
