Last updated: July 2026

Applies to all users: customers, shop owners, staff, and visitors.

1. Prohibited conduct

Fraud, fake payments, chargeback abuse, listing counterfeit or illegal goods, harassment, hate speech, scraping that harms the service, attempting to bypass security, or impersonating DPM or another shop.

2. Shop-specific

False "paid" confirmations, refusing fulfilment after confirmed payment, bait-and-switch pricing, or misusing customer contact details.

3. Customer-specific

False reports, abusive messages to shops, or repeated frivolous disputes.

4. Enforcement

We may remove content, issue cautions (with required acknowledgment), restrict checkout or selling, or suspend accounts. Automation may apply only when enabled by administrators.

5. Cooperation

We may cooperate with law enforcement where required by Ghana law.

6. Reporting

Customers: report a shop from the order detail page (order required).

Everyone: contact us for urgent safety issues via the Contact page.
