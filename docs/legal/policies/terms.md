1. About DanyPathMart

DanyPathMart ("DPM", "we", "us") operates an online platform in Ghana for school supplies and related goods. We sell products directly as DPM and host verified independent shops on their own storefront links.

By using our website, mobile experience, or seller tools, you agree to these Terms of Service and to our Privacy Policy, Payment Policy, Returns & Refunds Policy, and other published policies linked on the site.

2. Two ways to buy

DPM catalog: Products sold by DanyPathMart (no shop seller) are purchased on the main site (/shop). Payment is collected by DPM through approved methods (e.g. Paystack, wallet, bank transfer, or pay-on-delivery where offered).

Shop storefronts: Products sold by independent shops are purchased only through that shop's link (/stores/{shop-slug}). Payment is collected by the shop using methods the shop enables (Paystack subaccount, mobile money, or in-person payment). DPM does not take commission on shop product sales. Shops pay a registration and/or subscription fee to keep their storefront link active.

4. Shop sellers

Shops must apply, be verified, and maintain an active subscription where billing is enabled. Shop owners agree to the Shop Seller Policy and Seller Handbook. If a subscription lapses, the public shop link is hidden until renewal.

5. Pricing and availability

Prices are shown in Ghana Cedis (GHS) unless stated otherwise. We and shop sellers may change listings, prices, and availability. An order is confirmed when payment is completed (or placed under pay-on-delivery rules for eligible DPM orders).

6. Our role

For DPM catalog orders, DPM is the merchant of record for platform checkout.

For shop storefront orders, the independent shop is responsible for fulfillment, payment confirmation (where manual), and customer service for that order, except where DPM provides platform moderation (reports, cautions, suspension).

Customers may report a shop only in connection with an order they placed. We may issue notices, cautions, or restrictions to any user, and suspend shops or accounts for policy breaches.
